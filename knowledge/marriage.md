## Pre-Wedding Services

### Kundli Milan (Horoscope Matching)
- Matching of birth charts of prospective bride and groom
- Ashtakoot system (36 gunas analysis)
- Minimum 18 gunas required for compatibility
- Detailed report on compatibility across:
  - Varna (spiritual compatibility)
  - Vasya (dominance and control)
  - Tara (health and well-being)
  - Yoni (sexual compatibility)
  - Graha Maitri (mental compatibility)
  - Gana (temperament)
  - Bhakoot (health and financial prosperity)
  - Nadi (health and genetics)

### Mangal Dosh Analysis
- Important to check before marriage
- Remedies if Mangal Dosh is present
- Mangal Shanti Pooja if required

### Vivah Muhurta (Auspicious Date Selection)
- Selection of the most auspicious date, time, and lagna for the wedding
- Based on both bride and groom's horoscopes
- Panchang (Hindu almanac) consultation
- Avoiding inauspicious periods (Raahu Kaal, etc.)

## Wedding Ceremony (Vivah Sanskar)

### Traditional Hindu Wedding Rituals

**Mandap Sthapana** — Setting up the sacred wedding canopy

**Ganesh Pooja** — Invoking Lord Ganesha for removal of obstacles

**Gauri Pooja** — Worship of Goddess Gauri for the bride

**Var Mala (Jaimala)** — Exchange of garlands between bride and groom

**Kanyadan** — Sacred ritual of the bride's father giving the daughter in marriage

**Saptapadi (Saat Phere)** — Seven steps/vows around the sacred fire
1. First step — for nourishment and food
2. Second step — for strength and prosperity
3. Third step — for wealth
4. Fourth step — for happiness
5. Fifth step — for children and progeny
6. Sixth step — for health and long life
7. Seventh step — for friendship and companionship

**Sindoor Daan** — Application of sindoor in the bride's hair parting

**Mangalsutra** — Tying of the sacred thread

**Vidai** — Farewell ceremony for the bride

### Additional Ceremonies

**Tilak/Sagan Ceremony** — Formal engagement ceremony

**Mehndi Ceremony** — Henna ceremony (guidance and blessings)

**Haldi Ceremony** — Turmeric ceremony for purification

**Griha Pravesh (After Wedding)** — Welcome ceremony at the groom's home

## Gotra Considerations
- Both bride and groom must be of different Gotra (ancestral lineage)
- In many traditions, certain Pravara combinations are also avoided
- Pandit Ji will verify Gotra compatibility during Kundli Milan

## Documentation Needed
For wedding ceremonies:
- Full names of bride and groom
- Date, time, and place of birth of both
- Gotra of both families
- Preferred wedding dates
- Location of ceremony
