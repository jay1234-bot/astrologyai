## About Astrology Services

पंडित हरिनाथ शास्त्री provides expert Vedic astrology consultations based on traditional Jyotish Shastra.

## Kundli (Birth Chart)

A Kundli (birth horoscope) is prepared based on:
- Date of birth
- Time of birth
- Place of birth

The Kundli shows the position of all 9 planets (Navagraha) at the time of birth and is used to predict:
- Life events and timing
- Career and profession
- Marriage and relationships
- Health
- Wealth and finances
- Education

## Types of Consultations

### Janma Kundli (Birth Chart)
Detailed analysis of the complete birth chart. Covers all aspects of life.

### Varshfal (Annual Horoscope)
Annual predictions based on solar return chart. Predicts events for the coming year.

### Prashna Kundli (Horary Astrology)
Answer to a specific question using the chart of the moment the question is asked.

### Kundli Milan (Horoscope Matching)
Used for marriage compatibility. Matches the birth charts of prospective bride and groom based on Ashtakoot system (36 gunas).

### Remedial Astrology
Based on planetary positions, appropriate remedies are suggested:
- Gemstones (Ratna)
- Mantras
- Yantras
- Specific poojas
- Charity (Daan)
- Fasting (Vrat)

## Navagraha (Nine Planets)

1. **Surya (Sun)** — Soul, father, authority, government
2. **Chandra (Moon)** — Mind, mother, emotions, creativity
3. **Mangal (Mars)** — Energy, courage, siblings, property
4. **Budha (Mercury)** — Intelligence, communication, business
5. **Guru (Jupiter)** — Wisdom, education, dharma, children
6. **Shukra (Venus)** — Love, marriage, beauty, arts
7. **Shani (Saturn)** — Discipline, karma, delays, longevity
8. **Rahu** — Ambition, foreign matters, technology (shadow planet)
9. **Ketu** — Spirituality, liberation, past karma (shadow planet)

## Common Dosha (Planetary Afflictions)

### Kaal Sarp Dosh
All planets fall between Rahu and Ketu. Can cause obstacles and delays. Remedial poojas are available.

### Mangal Dosh (Manglik)
Mars is in 1st, 4th, 7th, 8th, or 12th house. Important to consider for marriage matching.

### Shani Sade Sati
7.5-year transit of Saturn over the moon sign. Requires careful planning and remedies.
