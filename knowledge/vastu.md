## What is Vastu Shastra?

Vastu Shastra is the ancient Indian science of architecture and space design. It harmonizes living and working spaces with natural energies, directions, and the five elements (Pancha Bhuta):
- **Prithvi** (Earth)
- **Jal** (Water)
- **Agni** (Fire)
- **Vayu** (Air)
- **Akash** (Space/Ether)

## Types of Vastu Consultations

### Residential Vastu
For homes, flats, and independent houses:
- Entry/main door direction
- Bedroom placement
- Kitchen direction (ideal: south-east)
- Pooja room direction (ideal: north-east)
- Study room direction
- Bathroom/toilet placement

### Commercial Vastu
For offices, shops, and business establishments:
- Main entrance direction
- Cash counter placement
- Owner's cabin/seating direction
- Employee seating arrangement
- Storage and filing areas

### Plot & Land Vastu
Assessment of land before purchase or construction:
- Shape of the plot
- Direction of slopes
- Surrounding environment

### Industrial/Factory Vastu
For manufacturing units and factories:
- Main entrance
- Production area
- Office area
- Storage

### Vastu Remedies (Without Demolition)
If structural changes are not possible, remedies are provided using:
- Crystals and gemstones
- Yantras
- Plants
- Colors
- Mirrors
- Wind chimes
- Rearrangement of furniture
