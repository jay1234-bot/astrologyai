## About Pooja Services

पंडित हरिनाथ शास्त्री performs all types of Hindu poojas and religious ceremonies with proper Vedic rituals, mantras, and procedures according to traditional Shastra.

## Popular Poojas

### Satyanarayan Katha
- Dedicated to Lord Vishnu
- Performed on full moon days, festivals, or auspicious occasions
- Duration: Approximately 3-4 hours
- Purpose: Prosperity, family well-being, fulfillment of wishes

### Ganesh Pooja (Shodashopchara)
- Performed before any new beginning or auspicious event
- Removes obstacles (Vighnaharta)
- Duration: Approximately 1-2 hours

### Griha Pravesh Pooja (Housewarming)
- Performed before entering a new home
- Purifies the space and invites divine blessings
- Duration: Approximately 3-4 hours
- Includes: Navgraha Pooja, Vastu Shanti, Griha Devata Sthapana

### Navgraha Shanti Pooja
- Pooja for the nine planets (Navagraha)
- Performed to pacify malefic planets
- Duration: Approximately 2-3 hours
- Beneficial for: Removing planetary doshas

### Laxmi Pooja
- Dedicated to Goddess Laxmi (wealth and prosperity)
- Especially performed on Diwali, Fridays, or for business success
- Duration: Approximately 1-2 hours

### Saraswati Pooja
- Dedicated to Goddess Saraswati (knowledge and learning)
- Performed during Vasant Panchami and before exams
- Beneficial for: Students and those seeking knowledge

### Kaal Sarp Dosh Pooja
- Performed to mitigate Kaal Sarp Dosh in the horoscope
- Special mantras and rituals for Rahu-Ketu
- Duration: Approximately 2-3 hours

### Mangal Dosh Shanti
- Performed for individuals with Mangal (Mars) dosha
- Important before marriage for manglik individuals
- Duration: Approximately 2 hours

### Rudrabhishek
- Sacred bathing ritual of Shivling
- Performed with Panchamrit (milk, curd, honey, ghee, sugar water)
- Duration: Approximately 2 hours

## Katha (Religious Discourses)

### Sunderkand Path
- Recitation of Sunderkand from Ramcharitmanas or Valmiki Ramayan
- Duration: Approximately 3-4 hours (single session) or multiple sessions
- Benefits: Removes obstacles, grants wishes, family harmony

### Ramayan Path (Akhand Ramayan)
- Complete recitation of Ramcharitmanas
- Duration: 24 hours (continuous/Akhand) or spread over multiple days
- Requires multiple Pandits for Akhand recitation

### Shrimad Bhagwat Katha
- 7-day discourse on the Bhagwat Purana
- Conducted by experienced Katha Vachak
- Includes daily pooja and aarti

### Vishnu Sahasranama Path
- Recitation of 1000 names of Lord Vishnu
- Duration: Approximately 1-1.5 hours
- Can be done daily or on special occasions

## What is Included in a Pooja Service?
